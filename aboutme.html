My name is Sam Martin. As of 2026, I am a year 10 student studying at Burnside High School. As you probably guessed from this website, I am taking the 10DGT course and have been learning about different aspects of digital technology throughout the year. I enjoy learning, and this course has taught me about the digital world that we live in today.

Aside from digital technology, I enjoy math, science, and music. I am a member of the Specialist Music Programme (SMP) at BHS, playing the oboe in the school orchestra, symphonic band, junior concert band, and chamber groups throughout the year. Other school groups that I am involved in include the chess club, philosphy club, and D&D club.

One of my common pasttimes is reading. I enjoy science fiction and fantasy novels, with some of my facourite authors being Isaac Asimov, J.R.R Tolkien, and Andy Weir. I also enjoy everything to do with RPN calculators, and have enjoyed programming my father's HP42s that he used when he was in school.
