How to play

  • You must find the image and its title through a web search.
  • Text-based searches are allowed.
  • Reverse-image searches and all other non-text-based searches are not allowed.
  • Once you find the image, login and paste the title of the image into the submission box.
  • After submitting, if you are correct, you will earn points. Otherwise, keep searching!
